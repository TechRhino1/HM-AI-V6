# M1 study: does an M1 entry make money after costs? (2026-11-17)

**Request:** "please find M1 TRADING ALGORITHUM and calculation also". This is plan v3, Phase 3: cost viability first, then the strategy.

## Short answer
**No.** No M1 configuration passes, so no M1 logic goes into live code.

- **Cost gate.** Entry spread + slippage must be ≤ 15% of the setup's own stop.
  - **Forex fails:** EURUSD 28%, GBPUSD 23%.
  - **BTCUSD fails:** 16%.
  - **Only XAUUSD passes:** 4%.
  - The same setups on M5 cost about 2.5× less per unit of stop. M1 roughly doubles to triples the cost burden.
- **Strategy gate.** Even before costs are a factor, every symbol loses on the base M1 setup.
  - The base setup is displacement + FVG + CHoCH, E1 entry, 2.5R target.
  - Forex: −0.31R per trade. Crypto: −0.10R. Gold: −0.03R.
  - Gold's best variant is the session filter alone: +0.15R, PF 1.22, n = 97. It made all its money in the first half: +0.60R and +1.10R in folds 1–2, then −0.11R and −0.04R in folds 3–4. That is decay, not an edge.
- **A higher win rate is the usual trap.** Partials (X1) lift the win rate from about 25% to 41–52%, but expectancy improves only for gold (+0.05R, PF 1.09), which is below the PF 1.1 gate.

## Method
**Tool:** `tools/m1_research.py`, a wrapper on the unchanged `tools/smc_research.py`. Same honesty rules:
- completed bars only;
- next-open entry at the real per-bar spread;
- slippage on stop exits, plus swap;
- stop assumed hit first when it shares a bar with a target;
- benchmarks: flipped, always-long and spread × 2.

**Setup:**
- One added style: M1 entry, bias from completed M15 + H1 bars.
- Every rule, filter, exit (X0–X4b), the 4 calendar folds and the adoption gate (`sr.verdict`, PF > 1.5, ≥ 3/4 folds positive) are identical to the M5–H1 study.

**Data:**
- Real MT5 M1 bars, as many as the broker serves (100k bars).
- XAUUSD 103 days; EURUSD and GBPUSD 98 days; BTCUSD 70 days (Aug/Sep to Nov 2026).
- That is the whole M1 history available, so the samples are small.

## Cost gate: median (spread + slippage) / stop, over every setup
| Symbol | M1 setups | M1 cost/stop (median, p75) | M5 cost/stop (median) | M1 verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| XAUUSD | 881 | **4.0%**, 5.4% | 1.4% | pass |
| EURUSD | 840 | **28.4%**, 39.8% | 13.0% | fail |
| GBPUSD | 837 | **23.3%**, 32.8% | 8.8% | fail |
| BTCUSD | 1,215 | **15.9%**, 25.5% | 7.4% | fail |

At 28% cost/stop, a 2.5R winner nets about 2.2R while every loser costs 1.28R. That needs a win rate above about 37% just to break even. The base M1 setup wins 22–28%.

| gold | X0 + session filter only | 97 | 33% | +0.147 | 1.22 | +0.60 / +1.10 / −0.11 / −0.04 | +0.147 |

| crypto | X0 | 394 | 28% | −0.097 | 0.88 | all four negative | −0.160 |

- **All filters stacked:** 0–5 trades per group in 3 months, so it can't be tested.
- **Every filter on its own** made expectancy worse or left it unchanged in every group.
- **By regime:** gold `HIGH_VOL_SHOCK` (+0.12R, n = 25) and gold `TREND` (+0.04R, n = 362) are the only positive regime cells. Neither is large or strong enough to act on.

## What this means
- **No M1 entry engine.** Do not move the scalp timing frame onto M1 entries.
- **Forex and crypto:** M1 is ruled out by cost alone.
- **Gold M1:** possible on cost, but it showed no edge here.
- **Re-test gold M1** only when about a year of M1 history exists. That means recording M1 bars daily from now on, because the broker serves only about 100 days.
- The live SCALP style keeps M1 as a *timing* frame only. Phase 0 #19 made that frame read closed bars only.
